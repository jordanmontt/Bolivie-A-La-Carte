Guayaramerín

Beni Department · Cities

Guayaramerín is a lively little border town on the wide Mamoré river. You cross by boat to Brazil, to Guajará-Mirim. With Riberalta, it's the base for Cachuela Esperanza and its rapids, and for the very untouristy northern Amazon.

Things to do

  • Cross the Mamoré to Guajará-Mirim (Brazil)
  • Cachuela Esperanza and its rapids
  • Walk along the Mamoré

What to eat

  • River fishSurubí, pacú or dorado, grilled over a wood fire or fried, with rice, cassava and plantain. Delicious by the water, in the Llanos and the Amazon.
  • MasacoMashed plantain or cassava with charque or cheese. Rich and filling.
Tip. 1 h 30 from Riberalta by shared taxi or bus. To cross into Brazil, keep your passport with you.

Also here

  • Border Guayaramerín – Guajará-MirimBy boat across the Mamoré river, in a few minutes. Formalities are done on each side of the river.

How to get to Guayaramerín

  • Riberalta Road (bus, car) · 1 h 30

    Shared taxi or bus, paved road.
