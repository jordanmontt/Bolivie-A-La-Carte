Beni Biosphere Reserve

Beni Department · Jungle and tropics

Near San Borja, the Beni Biosphere Reserve and its biological station protect flooded savannas, forest islands and lagoons. You can see hundreds of bird species, monkeys and caimans, with park rangers or local guides. A very wild Amazon, for patient travellers.

Things to do

  • Birdwatching in the savannas
  • Lagoons, caimans and monkeys
  • Outings with the park rangers

What to eat

  • Beni beefThe Beni is cattle country: big grills (churrasco) and dried meat.
Tip. Reached via San Borja, to arrange there (car with driver or guide). Best in the dry season.

How to get to Beni reserve

  • Rurrenabaque Road (bus, car) · 5 to 7 h

    Bus to San Borja, then transport arranged there.

    Dirt road, hard going in the rainy season.

  • RiberaltaAn Amazon river port where the Beni meets the Madre de Dios, capital of the Brazil nut.
  • GuayaramerínA border town on the Mamoré river, facing the Brazilian town of Guajará-Mirim.
