San Jacinto lake and Tomatitas

Tarija Department · Nature and hiking

The San Jacinto reservoir, a few minutes from Tarija, can be enjoyed by boat or on foot along the shore, with fish restaurants by the water. On the other side of town, Tomatitas is the river where families come to swim and picnic on Sundays.

Things to do

  • Boat ride on San Jacinto lake
  • Fish and cangrejitos by the water
  • Swimming at Tomatitas

What to eat

  • River fishGrilled surubí, pacú or dorado, with cassava and fried plantain.
  • CangrejitosSmall crab-shaped pastries from Tarija, filled with cream. A local dessert.
Tip. Taxi or minibus from Tarija. Busy on Sundays.
