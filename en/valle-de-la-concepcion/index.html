Valle de la Concepción

Tarija Department · Culture and history

Around Tarija, and especially in the Concepción valley (Uriondo), the bodegas open their doors for wine and singani tastings. Some are large modern wineries, others small family houses. A perfect day for wine lovers.

1,700 maltitude
1 dayto spend there
Open on the interactive mapGoogle Maps

Things to do

  • Tastings at the region's bodegas (Kohlberg, Aranjuez, Campos de Solana…)
  • The village of Concepción

What to eat

  • Tarija wineHigh-altitude wines (up to 2,000 m): tannat, malbec, muscat. Tastings at the valley's bodegas.
  • SinganiA muscat grape brandy and a national pride. Drunk as a “chuflay” (with ginger ale).
  • SaiceA spicy stew of minced beef and peas, served with rice and potatoes. Tarija's signature dish.
Tip. Day trip from Tarija, on a tour or by taxi.

How to get to Tarija wineries

  • Tarija Road (bus, car) · 40 min

    Taxi or winery tour.

More places in this region

  • TarijaBolivia's Andalusia: high-altitude vineyards, singani and a relaxed vibe.
  • San LorenzoA colonial Chapaco village, home of the hero Moto Méndez, 15 km from Tarija.
  • CoimataA river, natural pools and a tall waterfall, 20 minutes from Tarija.
  • San Jacinto lake and TomatitasA lake among the hills and a river for swimming, on Tarija's doorstep.
  • Padcaya and ChaguayaA valley village south of Tarija, and the great sanctuary of the Virgin of Chaguaya.
  • BermejoA tropical border town on the Bermejo river, facing Argentina.
  • Sama reserve: Tajzara lagoonsHigh lagoons, dunes and flamingos in the Sama reserve, above Tarija.
  • Tariquía reserveCloud forests and wild rivers, for multi-day treks with a guide.
  • Camargo (Cinti valley)Old vineyards and red cliffs in the Cinti valley, between Potosí and Tarija.
  • Villamontes (Gran Chaco)Gateway to the Gran Chaco, on the Pilcomayo river and its canyon, El Angosto.
  • YacuibaThe Chaco's big border town, facing Argentina.
  • CamiriThe Chaco's "oil capital", among hills and Guaraní culture.
