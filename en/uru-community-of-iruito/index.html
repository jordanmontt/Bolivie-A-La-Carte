Uru community of Iruito

La Paz Department · Culture and history

The Urus are one of the oldest peoples of the Andes, long called the "people of the water". At Iruito, on the Desaguadero river, their community has opened a small museum and welcomes visitors to talk about their history, fishing and reeds. A rare encounter, to be approached with respect.

High altitude. Above 3,500 m, altitude sickness (soroche) is common. Arrive gradually, drink plenty and take it slow on the first day.

Things to do

  • Uru community museum
  • Trip on the Desaguadero river
  • Meeting the community

What to eat

  • ChuñoPotatoes freeze-dried in the sun, an Andean technique thousands of years old. A surprising taste, worth trying at least once.
Tip. Reached via Jesús de Machaca, best with a guide. Ask before taking photos.

How to get to Iruito

  • Jesús de Machaca Road (bus, car) · 45 min

    Local transport or car, best with a guide.
