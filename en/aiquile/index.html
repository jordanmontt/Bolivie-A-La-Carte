Aiquile

Cochabamba Department · Culture and history

Aiquile is a small, warm and dry valley town in the south of Cochabamba department. It is known across the country as the national capital of the charango, the small ten-string Andean guitar: its luthiers have made them for generations, and there's a museum devoted to it. In early November, the International Charango Fair and Festival brings together musicians and makers from all over Bolivia. A pleasant, very local stop on the road between Cochabamba and Sucre.

Things to do

  • National Charango Museum

  • The Charango Fair and Festival, early November
  • Cathedral and main square

What to eat

  • Picante mixtoA plate of several meats (chicken, tongue, beef) in chilli sauce, with chuño and rice. For chilli lovers.
  • ChicharrónPork cooked slowly in its own fat until golden, served with mote (big corn kernels) and chuño. A Sunday dish.
  • ChichaFermented corn beer, slightly sour, served in gourds (tutumas). Look for chicherías marked with a white flag.
Tip. Few tourists and simple lodging. For the festival, book early and check the dates with the town hall.

Also here

  • Festivals Aiquile charango festivalIn early November, musicians and luthiers from all over Bolivia meet in the charango capital for competitions and concerts.
  • Crafts and markets Aiquile charangosThe small ten-string Andean guitar, made by Aiquile's luthiers. You can order one made to measure.

How to get to Aiquile

  • Mizque Road (bus, car) · 1 h 30

    Minibus or bus.

  • Cochabamba Road (bus, car) · 4 to 5 h

    Buses and minibuses from Cochabamba, a few departures a day.

  • Sucre Road (bus, car) · 5 to 6 h

    Cochabamba – Sucre buses pass through Aiquile: ask to get on or off.
