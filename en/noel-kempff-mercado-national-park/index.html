Noel Kempff Mercado National Park

Santa Cruz Department · Jungle and tropics

Noel Kempff Mercado is one of the wildest places in South America: table-top plateaus, spectacular waterfalls and exceptional wildlife. It is said to have inspired Conan Doyle's “The Lost World”. Very hard to reach and expensive: an expedition in its own right.

What to eat

  • River fishGrilled surubí, pacú or dorado, with cassava and fried plantain.
  • MasacoMashed plantain or cassava with charque or cheese. Rich and filling.
Tip. An expedition: light aircraft or a 12-hour-plus dirt track. Expensive, to organise in advance. Allow a week, including travel.
