Amboró National Park

Santa Cruz Department · Jungle and tropics

Amboró is one of the most biodiverse parks on the continent: cloud forests, tree ferns several metres tall, toucans and cocks-of-the-rock. You explore it on foot with a guide from Samaipata. It feels like walking through a prehistoric landscape.

Things to do

  • Giant fern trail (La Yunga sector)
  • Refugio Los Volcanes
  • Birds: cocks-of-the-rock, toucans

What to eat

  • MajaditoRice cooked with charque or chicken, served with a fried egg and fried plantain. The dish of Santa Cruz and the Beni.
  • Tropical fruitMangoes, pineapples, papayas, achachairú (a small tangy fruit) and fresh juices everywhere for a few bolivianos.
Tip. Access from Samaipata with a guide.

How to get to Amboró

  • Samaipata 4x4 tour or excursion · 1 h by 4x4
