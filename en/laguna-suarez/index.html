Laguna Suárez

Beni Department · Nature and hiking

A few kilometres from Trinidad, Laguna Suárez is where locals come to cool off: swimming, boat rides, birds and Beni fish restaurants by the water. A good introduction to life on the Moxos plains.

150 maltitude
0 to 1 dayto spend there
Open on the interactive mapGoogle Maps

Things to do

  • Swimming and boating on the lagoon
  • Beni fish by the water
  • Birds at sunset

What to eat

  • River fishSurubí, pacú or dorado, grilled over a wood fire or fried, with rice, cassava and plantain. Delicious by the water, in the Llanos and the Amazon.
Tip. 10 min from Trinidad by motorbike taxi or taxi.

How to get to Laguna Suárez

  • Trinidad Road (bus, car) · 10 min

    Motorbike taxi or taxi from Trinidad.
