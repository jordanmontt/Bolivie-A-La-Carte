Missions: San Javier

Santa Cruz Department · Culture and history

San Javier is the first of the UNESCO-listed Jesuit missions of Chiquitos: 18th-century painted wooden churches built by the Jesuits and the Chiquitano peoples in the middle of the forest. A peaceful village and a lovely introduction to the missions circuit.

Things to do

  • Restored church from 1752

What to eat

  • Chiquitos cheesesFresh cow's milk cheeses made in the mission villages, with homemade bread.
  • Cuñapé and pan de arrozLittle cheese breads made with cassava or rice flour, served warm with coffee in the morning.
  • Locro de gallinaA thick rice soup with free-range chicken, comforting and typical of the east.
Tip. First stop on the missions circuit from Santa Cruz.

Also here

  • UNESCO Jesuit Missions of the Chiquitos
  • Myths and legends The JichiIn the eastern lowlands, the Jichi is the guardian spirit of water, often a snake or a jaguar. It is said that if it isn't respected, wells and lagoons dry up.

How to get to San Javier
