Salar de Uyuni + Sud Lípez (tour)

Potosí Department · Altiplano, lakes and salt flats

The Salar de Uyuni is 10,000 km² of white salt as far as the eye can see, a perfectly flat horizon where you lose all sense of scale: it's the place for impossible photos and, in the rainy season, the sky reflects as if in a giant mirror. The tour continues into the Sud Lípez, with red and green lagoons full of flamingos, steaming geysers at sunrise and a hot bath at 4,400 m. For many people, it's the strongest memory of a trip to Bolivia.

High altitude. Above 3,500 m, altitude sickness (soroche) is common. Arrive gradually, drink plenty and take it slow on the first day.

Things to do

  • Day 1: Colchani, a salt hotel, Isla Incahuasi with its giant cacti, perspective photos
  • Day 2: flamingo lagoons, Árbol de Piedra, Laguna Colorada
  • Day 3: Sol de Mañana geysers at dawn, a soak at Termas de Polques, Laguna Verde
  • Back to Uyuni (or on to San Pedro de Atacama in Chile)

What to eat

  • Quinoa realThe world's best quinoa grows around the Salar: in soups, patties or as a side.
  • Llama meatLean and tasty meat, often grilled or as “charque” (dried). Very common in Uyuni and on the Altiplano.
  • Quinoa soupA hot soup of quinoa and vegetables, served in refuges and high-altitude comedores. Perfect when it's cold.
Tip. 4x4 tour of 1 to 4 days (3 days recommended). Up to 5,000 m with nights at −10 °C: bring a sleeping bag and warm clothes. Rainy season (January to March): mirror effect, but some areas are closed.

Also here

  • Myths and legends Tunupa and the SalarThe mountain Tunupa, abandoned by her husband, wept while nursing her child: her tears and milk are said to have formed the Salar de Uyuni, which she still overlooks to the north.
  • Border Hito Cajón (to San Pedro de Atacama)At the end of the Sud Lípez tour, near Laguna Verde: many tours drop travellers here, with a transfer to San Pedro de Atacama (about 1 h). Book it with the tour.

How to get to Salar de Uyuni

  • Uyuni 4x4 tour or excursion · Included in the tour

    4x4 tour with a driver-guide and a cook. Book in Uyuni (compare agencies) or in advance.

  • Tupiza 4x4 tour or excursion · Included (4-day tour)

    A version of the tour that starts in Tupiza and ends in Uyuni, crossing the Sud Lípez.

More places in this region

  • Sajama National ParkBolivia's highest peak (6,542 m), geysers, hot springs and llamas.
  • Curahuara de CarangasThe “Sistine Chapel of the Altiplano”: an adobe church covered in murals.
  • OruroAn Altiplano mining city, famous for its UNESCO-listed carnival. Starting point of the train to Uyuni.
  • Obrajes and Capachos hot springsNatural hot-water pools on the Altiplano, less than an hour from Oruro.
  • Machacamarca railway museumOld steam locomotives and the history of the mining railways, south of Oruro.
  • Lakes Uru Uru and PoopóShallow Altiplano lakes on Oruro's doorstep, visited by flamingos.
  • ChipayaVillage of the Uru-Chipaya, one of the oldest peoples of the Andes, with round earthen houses.
  • Coipasa Salt FlatBolivia's second-largest salt flat, wild and almost without visitors.
  • UyuniBase town for Salar tours, with a train cemetery on the edge of town.
  • TupizaWild West scenery: red canyons, cacti and the last ride of Butch Cassidy.
  • CotagaitaA small green town in a fruit-growing valley, on the road from Potosí to Villazón.
  • VillazónThe border with Argentina, facing La Quiaca: the crossing travellers use most.
