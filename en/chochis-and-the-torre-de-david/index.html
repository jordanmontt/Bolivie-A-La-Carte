Chochís and the Torre de David

Santa Cruz Department · Nature and hiking

In Chochís, a gigantic red sandstone cliff, the Torre de David, towers over the forest. At its foot, a sanctuary built after a natural disaster blends into the rock. You can climb to the top for a magnificent view of the Tucavaca valley, then swim under the waterfalls.

Things to do

  • Chochís Marian sanctuary, built at the foot of the cliff
  • Climb the Torre de David (view over the whole valley)
  • Waterfalls and swimming in the Tucavaca valley

What to eat

  • Locro de gallinaA thick rice soup with free-range chicken, comforting and typical of the east.
  • MasacoMashed plantain or cassava with charque or cheese. Rich and filling.
  • Tropical fruitMangoes, pineapples, papayas, achachairú (a small tangy fruit) and fresh juices everywhere for a few bolivianos.
Tip. On the road and railway between San José and Roboré. Best with a local guide from Roboré.

How to get to Chochís

  • Roboré Road (bus, car) · 1 h

    Taxi or tour from Roboré.

  • San José Road (bus, car) · 1 h 30

    Bus towards Roboré or Puerto Quijarro (get off at the junction), or taxi.
