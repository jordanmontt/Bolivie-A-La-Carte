Laguna Alalay

Cochabamba Department · Nature and hiking

In the south of Cochabamba, Laguna Alalay is a wide stretch of water ringed with reeds, where many birds live or stop over. A path runs around it, with the Cristo de la Concordia and the mountains as a backdrop. A green breather in the middle of the city, best early in the morning.

Things to do

  • Walk or cycle around the lagoon
  • Birdwatching
  • View of the Cristo de la Concordia

What to eat

  • SilpanchoA thin breaded beef cutlet on rice and potatoes, topped with a fried egg and a tomato and onion salad. THE Cochabamba dish.
Tip. By taxi or micro from the centre. Best in the morning, in daylight.

How to get to Laguna Alalay
