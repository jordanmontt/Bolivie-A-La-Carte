Cotoca

Santa Cruz Department · Culture and history

Cotoca is a small town right next to Santa Cruz, known for the sanctuary of the Virgin of Cotoca, patron saint of the people of Santa Cruz. On 8 December, thousands of pilgrims walk there through the night. All year round you'll find local potters' ceramics and Santa Cruz specialities.

Things to do

  • Sanctuary of the Virgin of Cotoca
  • Cotoca ceramics and potters
  • Cuñapés and sonso on the square

What to eat

  • Cuñapé and pan de arrozLittle cheese breads made with cassava or rice flour, served warm with coffee in the morning.
  • Sonso de yucaCassava and cheese dough grilled on a skewer. A speciality of the east, eaten hot.
Tip. 30 min from Santa Cruz by micro or taxi.

Also here

  • Festivals Virgin of CotocaOn the night of 7 to 8 December, thousands of pilgrims walk from Santa Cruz to the sanctuary of the patron saint of the people of Santa Cruz, with mass, music and a fair.
  • Crafts and markets Cotoca potteryJugs, pots and terracotta figures made by Cotoca's potters, right next to Santa Cruz. You'll find them around the sanctuary.

How to get to Cotoca

  • Santa Cruz Road (bus, car) · 30 min

    Micro or taxi from Santa Cruz.
