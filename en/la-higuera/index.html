La Higuera

Santa Cruz Department · Culture and history

La Higuera is a tiny mountain village, still almost as it was in October 1967 when Ernesto “Che” Guevara was executed in its school. The old school is now a small museum, and the village is covered in murals and monuments. You can walk to the Quebrada del Yuro, where he was captured.

What to eat

  • MajaditoRice cooked with charque or chicken, served with a fried egg and fried plantain. The dish of Santa Cruz and the Beni.
  • FricaséA spicy pork soup with yellow chilli and mote, said to cure hangovers.
Tip. Dirt track from Vallegrande (about 60 km). Very simple lodging in the village.

How to get to La Higuera

  • Vallegrande Road (bus, car) · 2 to 3 h

    Shared taxi or car with driver from Vallegrande.

    Dirt track, hard going after rain.
