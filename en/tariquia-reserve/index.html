Tariquía reserve

Tarija Department · Nature and hiking

The Tariquía reserve protects humid mountain forests covered in moss and bromeliads, crossed by rivers you can swim in. There's almost no infrastructure: you go in on foot, with a guide, for several days of walking among forests, waterfalls and small communities. A real adventure for nature lovers.

Things to do

  • Multi-day trek in the cloud forest
  • Rivers and waterfalls
  • Birds and forest wildlife
  • Farming communities in the reserve

What to eat

  • SaiceA spicy stew of minced beef and peas, served with rice and potatoes. Tarija's signature dish.
  • River fishSurubí, pacú or dorado, grilled over a wood fire or fried, with rice, cassava and plantain. Delicious by the water, in the Llanos and the Amazon.
Tip. Difficult access and a SERNAP permit required: go with a Tarija agency. Best season: May to September.

How to get to Tariquía

  • Tarija 4x4 tour or excursion · 3 to 4 h to the entrance

    With a Tarija agency, then on foot.
