Camiri

Santa Cruz Department · Cities

Camiri is a small Chaco town at the foot of the last Andean hills, born with oil in the 20th century. It's also Guaraní land, and the gateway to the area where Che led his guerrilla campaign in 1967, towards Lagunillas and the Ñancahuazú river. A stop between Santa Cruz and the Chaco.

Things to do

  • Town and Chaco hills
  • Guaraní culture of the region
  • History of the 1967 guerrilla (Lagunillas, Ñancahuazú)

What to eat

  • MajaditoRice cooked with charque or chicken, served with a fried egg and fried plantain. The dish of Santa Cruz and the Beni.
  • Locro de gallinaA thick rice soup with free-range chicken, comforting and typical of the east.
Tip. Buses from Santa Cruz (about 5 h) or from Villamontes. Very hot in summer.

How to get to Camiri

  • Villamontes Road (bus, car) · about 3 h

    Bus towards Villamontes and Yacuiba.

  • Santa Cruz Road (bus, car) · about 5 h

    Bus from Santa Cruz's Bimodal terminal.
