Zudáñez

Chuquisaca Department · Culture and history

Zudáñez is named after Jaime Zudáñez, one of the leaders of the 1809 Chuquisaca uprising. The region was the scene of the independence guerrillas, and the little town keeps their memory. Today it's a quiet valley stop, with old streets and farmland all around.

What to eat

  • ChichaFermented corn beer, slightly sour, served in gourds (tutumas). Look for chicherías marked with a white flag.
  • Chorizo chuquisaqueñoSucre's fragrant pork sausage, grilled and served in a bread roll or with potatoes.
Tip. Buses from Sucre via Tarabuco. Little lodging: usually a return trip.

More places in this region

  • PotosíOnce the richest city in the world thanks to the silver of Cerro Rico. UNESCO-listed.
  • Casa Nacional de Moneda (Potosí)The royal mint where Cerro Rico silver became coins, now Bolivia's great museum.
  • Ojo del Inca (Tarapaya)A perfectly round warm-water lagoon, 30 minutes from Potosí.
  • SucreThe “white city” and constitutional capital: the most beautiful colonial city in the country.
  • TarabucoBig Sunday market of the Yampara communities, with remarkable textiles.
  • CandelariaA Yampara village beyond Tarabuco: an old hacienda, weaving and rural tourism.
  • Maragua CraterA giant syncline in reds and purples, with Jalq'a weaving villages.
