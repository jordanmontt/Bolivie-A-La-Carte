Santiago de Chiquitos

Santa Cruz Department · Nature and hiking

Santiago de Chiquitos is an old mission village perched on a plateau, with low houses around a large grassy square. From here you hike to breathtaking viewpoints over the Tucavaca valley, caves with rock paintings and stone arches. The village is cooler than the plains, and truly timeless.

Things to do

  • Mirador del Valle de Tucavaca at sunset
  • Cueva de Juan Miserandino and rock paintings
  • Arco de Piedra and waterfalls
  • Mission church

What to eat

  • Cuñapé and pan de arrozLittle cheese breads made with cassava or rice flour, served warm with coffee in the morning.
  • Locro de gallinaA thick rice soup with free-range chicken, comforting and typical of the east.
  • Chiquitos cheesesFresh cow's milk cheeses made in the mission villages, with homemade bread.
Tip. Cooler than Roboré thanks to the altitude. Guides and small hotels in the village.

How to get to Santiago de Chiquitos

  • Roboré Road (bus, car) · 45 min

    Taxi or micro (minibus) from Roboré.
