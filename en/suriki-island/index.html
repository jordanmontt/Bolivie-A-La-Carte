Suriki Island

La Paz Department · Culture and history

On the small island of Suriki, in the Lago Menor, Aymara families have built totora reed boats for generations. Craftsmen from the island helped explorer Thor Heyerdahl build Ra II, which crossed the Atlantic in 1970. A small museum and workshops tell this remarkable story.

High altitude. Above 3,500 m, altitude sickness (soroche) is common. Arrive gradually, drink plenty and take it slow on the first day.

Things to do

  • Totora boat workshops
  • Small museum of the Ra II expedition
  • Walk around the island, view of the cordillera

What to eat

  • TroutTrout from Lake Titicaca or the Cordillera rivers, fried or grilled, with rice, fries and salad. Eaten on the beach in Copacabana and in the small restaurants of mountain villages.
Tip. By boat from Huatajata or nearby villages. Combine with Pariti.

How to get to Suriki

  • Huatajata Boat · about 30 min
