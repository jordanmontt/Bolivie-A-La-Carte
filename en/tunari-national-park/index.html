Tunari National Park

Cochabamba Department · Nature and hiking

Right above the city, the Tunari range climbs past 5,000 m. In a single day you go from the eucalyptus of the valley to high-altitude lagoons with grazing llamas, with the whole Cochabamba valley at your feet. A great first hike to test your legs at altitude.

High altitude. Above 3,500 m, altitude sickness (soroche) is common. Arrive gradually, drink plenty and take it slow on the first day.

Things to do

  • Hike to the lagoons (Laguna Wara Wara, Lagunas de Tunari)
  • Climb Cerro Tunari if you're keen (long day)
  • Views over the whole Cochabamba valley

What to eat

  • SilpanchoA thin breaded beef cutlet on rice and potatoes, topped with a fried egg and a tomato and onion salad. THE Cochabamba dish.
  • Quinoa soupA hot soup of quinoa and vegetables, served in refuges and high-altitude comedores. Perfect when it's cold.
Tip. Day trip from Cochabamba, best with a guide or a 4x4. A good acclimatisation test.

How to get to Tunari Park

  • Cochabamba Road (bus, car) · 1 to 2 h

    4x4 taxi or guided trip. The tracks climb straight up north of the city.
