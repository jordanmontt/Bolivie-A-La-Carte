Laguna Volcán

Santa Cruz Department · Nature and hiking

Laguna Volcán is a quiet lake ringed by forested mountains that make it look like a crater. A trail runs around it, between forest and cliffs, with plenty of birds. A calm nature stop on the road between Santa Cruz and Samaipata.

Things to do

  • Trail around the lagoon
  • Birds and forest
  • A stop on the road to Samaipata

What to eat

  • MajaditoRice cooked with charque or chicken, served with a fried egg and fried plantain. The dish of Santa Cruz and the Beni.
  • Tropical fruitMangoes, pineapples, papayas, achachairú (a small tangy fruit) and fresh juices everywhere for a few bolivianos.
Tip. The lagoon is on private land (a hotel): check the access conditions before you go.
