Missions: San José de Chiquitos

Santa Cruz Department · Culture and history

San José de Chiquitos has the only mission built of stone, an impressive complex facing a large square. The surrounding hills offer good walks. It's also the gateway to Chochís, Roboré and Aguas Calientes.

What to eat

  • Locro de gallinaA thick rice soup with free-range chicken, comforting and typical of the east.
  • MajaditoRice cooked with charque or chicken, served with a fried egg and fried plantain. The dish of Santa Cruz and the Beni.
  • Cuñapé and pan de arrozLittle cheese breads made with cassava or rice flour, served warm with coffee in the morning.
Tip. On the road and railway between Santa Cruz and Brazil: easy to get back.

How to get to San José

  • Chochís Road (bus, car) · 1 h 30

    Bus towards Roboré or Puerto Quijarro (get off at the junction), or taxi.

  • Roboré Road (bus, car) · 2 h 30

    Santa Cruz to Roboré or Puerto Quijarro buses that stop in San José.

  • Roboré Train · 3 h 30

    Ferroviaria Oriental train (Santa Cruz to Puerto Quijarro line).

  • Santa Cruz Road (bus, car) · 4 h 30

    Frequent buses.

  • San Miguel Road (bus, car) · 4 to 6 h

    Infrequent buses, or car.

  • Santa Cruz Train · 6 h

    Ferroviaria Oriental train (Santa Cruz to Puerto Quijarro line).
