San Ignacio de Moxos

Beni Department · Culture and history

San Ignacio de Moxos is the cultural capital of the Llanos de Moxos, the vast flood plains of the Beni. The former Jesuit mission has kept a strong tradition of baroque music and dance. Every 31 July, for the feast of Saint Ignatius, the UNESCO-listed Ichapekene Piesta brings out the macheteros in their feather headdresses for a festival unlike any other in South America.

What to eat

  • Beni beefThe Beni is cattle country: big grills (churrasco) and dried meat.
  • River fishSurubí, pacú or dorado, grilled over a wood fire or fried, with rice, cassava and plantain. Delicious by the water, in the Llanos and the Amazon.
  • MasacoMashed plantain or cassava with charque or cheese. Rich and filling.
Tip. 2 to 3 h from Trinidad on a dirt road, often hard going in the rainy season. For the festival, book well ahead.

Also here

  • Festivals · UNESCO Ichapekene PiestaThe great festival of San Ignacio de Moxos: the macheteros dance in huge feather headdresses, blending Mojeño traditions and the missions' heritage.
