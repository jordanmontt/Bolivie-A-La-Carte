Yacuiba

Tarija Department · Cities

Yacuiba, with its neighbour San José de Pocitos, is the main border town of the Bolivian Chaco, facing Salvador Mazza in Argentina. It's a lively trading town and a crossing point towards Salta. Most people pass through on journeys between Santa Cruz, Tarija and Argentina.

Things to do

  • Cross the border to Salvador Mazza (Argentina)
  • Markets and border trade
  • A stop between Santa Cruz and Argentina

What to eat

  • River fishSurubí, pacú or dorado, grilled over a wood fire or fried, with rice, cassava and plantain. Delicious by the water, in the Llanos and the Amazon.
  • Beni beefThe Beni is cattle country: big grills (churrasco) and dried meat.
Tip. Very hot in summer. Buses from Santa Cruz, Tarija and Villamontes.

Also here

  • Border Yacuiba – Salvador MazzaThe Chaco crossing, on the road from Santa Cruz to Salta. Direct buses from Santa Cruz, and international buses to Buenos Aires.

How to get to Yacuiba

  • Villamontes Road (bus, car) · 1 h 30

    Bus or shared taxi.

  • Santa Cruz Road (bus, car) · 8 to 10 h

    Night bus from Santa Cruz's Bimodal terminal.
