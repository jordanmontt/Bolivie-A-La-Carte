Sucre

Chuquisaca Department · Cities

Sucre is Bolivia's most beautiful city: white façades, tiled roofs, cloisters and shady squares in a valley with a mild climate. It's where independence was declared. You eat well, wander, climb to the Recoleta viewpoint and see a wall covered with thousands of dinosaur footprints. The ideal place to settle in for a few days.

Things to do

  • Recoleta viewpoint at sunset
  • Casa de la Libertad (independence)
  • Parque Cretácico: a wall with 5,000 dinosaur footprints (Cal Orck'o)
  • Central market (fruit juices, salteñas)
  • Rooftops of the San Felipe Neri convent

What to eat

  • Chorizo chuquisaqueñoSucre's fragrant pork sausage, grilled and served in a bread roll or with potatoes.
  • MondongoPork stew in red chilli sauce with mote, typical of Sucre at festival time.
  • Sucre chocolateSucre is the capital of Bolivian chocolate (Para Ti, Taboada): bars made with Amazonian cacao to take home.
  • SalteñaBolivia's pastry: slightly sweet dough with a juicy filling of meat, potato and olives. Eaten in the morning, without spilling the juice!
Tip. Mild climate and a pleasant city to slow down in. Lots of Spanish schools if you want to stay longer.

Also here

  • UNESCO Historic City of Sucre
  • Festivals · UNESCO Virgin of GuadalupeSucre's patron saint, celebrated in early September with a big folk parade through the streets of the historic centre.

How to get to Sucre

  • Cochabamba Flight · 35 min flight

    BoA flights to Alcantarí airport.

  • Santa Cruz Flight · 45 min flight

    BoA flights.

  • La Paz Flight · 55 min flight

    BoA flights.

  • Tarabuco Road (bus, car) · 1 h 30

    Tourist buses on Sunday mornings, or minibuses.

  • Maragua 4x4 tour or excursion · 2 h 30 by 4x4 or 1 to 3 days on foot

    Guided trek or 4x4 from Sucre.

  • Zudáñez Road (bus, car) · 2 to 3 h

    Bus from Sucre via Tarabuco.

  • Potosí Road (bus, car) · 3 h

    Buses or shared taxis (faster) from the Potosí bus terminal.

  • Candelaria Road (bus, car) · about 3 h

    Via Tarabuco, by bus then local transport, or with a Sucre agency.

  • Aiquile Road (bus, car) · 5 to 6 h

    Cochabamba – Sucre buses pass through Aiquile: ask to get on or off.

  • Cochabamba Road (bus, car) · 9 to 11 h (overnight)

    Night bus from the Cochabamba bus terminal.

    Long and winding: the 35-minute flight saves you a day.

  • Santa Cruz Road (bus, car) · 12 to 15 h (overnight)

    Night bus.
