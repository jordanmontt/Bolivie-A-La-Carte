Incallajta

Cochabamba Department · Culture and history

Incallajta is the largest Inca city in Bolivia, built to defend the empire against the peoples of the east. You'll find a huge ceremonial hall with stone and earth walls, in an isolated valley with hardly anyone around. A visit for history lovers and fans of places off the beaten track.

Things to do

  • The great kallanka (ceremonial hall) and the fortress
  • Nearby waterfall

What to eat

  • ChichaFermented corn beer, slightly sour, served in gourds (tutumas). Look for chicherías marked with a white flag.
  • Picante mixtoA plate of several meats (chicken, tongue, beef) in chilli sauce, with chuño and rice. For chilli lovers.
Tip. Little public transport: best by car or on an organised trip from Cochabamba. Can be combined with Totora.

How to get to Incallajta

  • Totora Road (bus, car) · 1 h 30

    Car.

  • Cochabamba Road (bus, car) · 3 h

    Car or organised trip. Public transport is tricky (bus to Pocona, then taxi).
