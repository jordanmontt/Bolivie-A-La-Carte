Vallegrande

Santa Cruz Department · Culture and history

Vallegrande is a quiet little colonial town high in the mountains west of Santa Cruz. It was here that Ernesto “Che” Guevara's body was displayed in 1967, in the hospital laundry, then secretly buried near the airstrip, where it was found in 1997. You can visit these places of memory, and the town is the base for going on to La Higuera.

What to eat

  • MajaditoRice cooked with charque or chicken, served with a fried egg and fried plantain. The dish of Santa Cruz and the Beni.
  • FricaséA spicy pork soup with yellow chilli and mote, said to cure hangovers.
  • Cuñapé and pan de arrozLittle cheese breads made with cassava or rice flour, served warm with coffee in the morning.
Tip. 3 h from Samaipata. Base for La Higuera: guides and taxis available.

How to get to Vallegrande

  • La Higuera Road (bus, car) · 2 to 3 h

    Shared taxi or car with driver from Vallegrande.

    Dirt track, hard going after rain.

  • Samaipata Road (bus, car) · 3 h

    Bus or “Che Route” tour from Samaipata.
